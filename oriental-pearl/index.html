东方明珠塔矗立于黄浦江畔，是上海城市天际线的重要地标。它像一串明珠，见证着上海从历史港埠迈向国际化都市。

Story

Standing by the Huangpu River, the Oriental Pearl Tower is a defining landmark of Shanghai's skyline. Like a string of shining pearls, it witnesses the city's journey from a historic port to a global metropolis.
